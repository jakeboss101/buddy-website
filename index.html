Buddy: Private AI Friend

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026 · Developer: Joseph Khan

The short version
  • Buddy runs entirely on your phone. Your chats never leave your phone (unless you open them on your own laptop with Buddy's Burrow, over your local Wi-Fi).
  • We don't collect any personal data. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your chats are never uploaded, never sold, and never used to train AI.
  • Buddy's data is kept out of iCloud and computer backups.
  • Web searches only happen when you say yes, and only the search words are sent.

1. Information we collect

None. We (the developer of Buddy) do not collect, receive, store, or have access to any of your personal information, conversations, photos, files, or usage data. Buddy has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking tools.

2. What Buddy stores on your phone

To be a friend who remembers you, Buddy saves the following only on your device, inside the app's private storage:

  • Your chat history with Buddy
  • Buddy's memory: the facts he has learned about you, and short summaries of past chats
  • Buddy's own backstory (which you can edit)
  • Photos and files you choose to share with Buddy
  • App settings and your best game score
  • Buddy's AI model files (his "brain" and "eyes")

This data is excluded from iCloud and computer backups, so it never leaves your phone that way either. As a result, if you move to a new phone, Buddy starts fresh.

4. Camera, photos, files, microphone and location

Buddy only accesses your camera, photo library, or files when you choose to share something. Shared photos and files are processed on your phone and stored only in Buddy's private storage. They are never uploaded.

When you talk to Buddy, your voice is turned into text right on your phone by Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your voice recordings are not saved or sent anywhere.

If you allow location access, Buddy uses it only when you ask about the weather or places near you. Your city name may be added to a web search you approve. Buddy never stores your location history.

5. Buddy's Burrow (optional)

Buddy's Burrow lets you chat with Buddy from a laptop's web browser, and invite friends as guests, over the same Wi-Fi network or your phone's Personal Hotspot. It is off unless you turn it on, a laptop or guest can only join with a one-time code shown on your phone, and it turns itself off after a while without use. Messages travel directly between your phone and those devices on your local network, never through the internet or through us. Guests can't see your private chats or what Buddy knows about you, and their chats aren't saved after the Burrow closes.

6. Apple

Buddy is distributed through Apple's App Store. Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics (such as the number of downloads), and only for users who chose to share analytics with app developers in their iPhone settings. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we cannot identify you from it.

7. About Buddy's AI

Buddy is an AI companion, not a person. His replies are generated on your phone and can sometimes be wrong. Buddy is not a doctor, therapist, lawyer, or emergency service, and his responses are not professional advice. If you are in crisis, please contact a real person: in the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or call 911 in an emergency. Outside the US, visit findahelpline.com. Buddy also shows these options in the app ("Need help now?").

8. Your control and deleting your data

  • See and edit everything Buddy remembers: tap Buddy's name in the chat to open his profile.
  • Erase everything: tap "UnFriend Buddy" in his profile. This deletes all chats, memories, summaries, shared photos and files, and your friendship level.
  • Delete the app to remove all of Buddy's data from your phone.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing stored on our side to request, export, or delete.

9. Security

Buddy's data is protected by your iPhone's built-in security and encryption. To keep it safe, use a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID on your phone. Anyone who can unlock your phone may be able to open Buddy and read your chats.

10. Children

Buddy is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how Buddy handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a future version adds any optional data sharing, it will be off by default, explained clearly in the app, and only turned on if you choose to.

Why does Buddy need to download about 6.5 GB?

That's Buddy's Brain (AI Model). Unlike cloud AI apps, Buddy runs entirely on your phone, so his AI model has to live on your phone too. He starts chatting after a smaller first download, then grows into bigger brains while you chat; each old brain is deleted when the new one arrives. After that, Buddy works without internet. Wi-Fi is recommended. You can leave the app while it downloads.

Does Buddy work offline?

Yes. After the one-time download, you can chat with Buddy even in airplane mode. Only optional web searches (which always ask you first) and weather need internet.

Why does Buddy take a nap?

Buddy can only hold so much of a conversation in his short-term memory. When it fills up, he takes a short nap to save what you talked about into his long-term memory, so he remembers you next time.

Which iPhones can run Buddy?

Buddy runs a full AI model on your phone, so he needs an iPhone 15 Pro or newer.

How do I see or change what Buddy remembers?

Tap Buddy's name at the top of the chat. In his profile, tap any fact to fix it or make him forget it.

I got a new phone. Where did Buddy's memories go?

For your privacy, Buddy's data is kept only on the phone you used, and not in backups. On a new phone, Buddy starts fresh.

How do I delete everything?

Open Buddy's profile and tap "UnFriend Buddy", or delete the app.
